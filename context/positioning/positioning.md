## Positioning statement
**For scale-ready businesses in Ghana that collect or send payments at high volume, Hubtel is the payments platform that removes what blocks payments across mobile money, cards, bank, POS, USSD and APIs, so they collect more of what they are owed. Unlike gateways built for checkout or for many countries, Hubtel has built in Ghana for 20 years and runs collections at the scale of a power distributor and a pan-African gaming company, according to its own case studies.**

- Basis: target and benefit from [icp] and [vo]; proof from [cu]; heritage and scale from [co].
- INFERRED: the contrast with "gateways built for checkout or for many countries". It is my reading of how competitors present themselves [cp], not a Hubtel claim.
- Caveats: the proof is self-reported and the customers are unnamed [cu]. Do not use this statement in outbound until Hubtel signs it off.
