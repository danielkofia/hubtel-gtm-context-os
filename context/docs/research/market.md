## Bank of Ghana: payment system rules
- Legal base: Payment Systems and Services Act, 2019 (Act 987) (https://www.bog.gov.gh/notice/licensing-and-authorisation-of-payment-service-providers/, 2026-11-16).
- Notice No. 43, issued 2026-11-06: "Management of Merchant Collection Funds by Settlement Banks and Payment Service Providers". Per summary: merchant collections must sit in designated settlement accounts at approved settlement banks, segregated from the PSP's operating funds; PSPs may not withdraw, transfer, pledge, lien or set off against them except to settle the merchant or move agreed fees; settlement banks must run the accounts solely for settlement; records and audit trails required. Implementation deadline or transition period: Not found. (https://www.bog.gov.gh/wp-content/uploads/2026/11/Notice-No.43-MANAGEMENT-OF-MERCHANT-COLLECTION-FUNDS-BY-SETTLEMENT-BANKS-AND-PAYMENT-SERVICE-PROVIDERS.pdf and https://www.graphic.com.gh/business/business-news/new-bog-directive-payment-service-providers-cannot-hold-merchant-funds.html, 2026-11-16). I did not open the PDF.
- Notice No. 41, 2026-11-05: Regulatory Framework for Dedicated Electronic Money Issuers (https://www.bog.gov.gh/wp-content/uploads/2026/11/Notice-No.41-BOG-SEC-GOV-2026-Regulatory-Framework-for-Dedicated-Electronic-Money-Issuers.pdf, 2026-11-16). Contents: Not found.
- Notice No. 42, 2026-11-06: Registered International Money Transfer Operators in Ghana (BoG page above). Contents: Not found.
- Framework for Systemically Important Payment Systems and Service Providers, 2026-11-05. Per summary, GhIPSS and MobileMoney Fintech LTD designated systemically important; designations reviewed at least every two years (BoG page above, 2026-11-16). Whether Hubtel is designated: Not found.
- Zeepay licence revoked July 2026 for e-money non-compliance; customers must be paid their e-money within ten days (https://www.myjoyonline.com/bank-of-ghana-revokes-zeepays-licence-over-e-money-violations/, 2026-11-16, search summary).
- BoG directed banks, EMIs and PSPs to stop supporting foreign-currency crypto wallets without authorisation under Acts 987 and 723 (https://www.myjoyonline.com/bank-of-ghana-orders-financial-institutions-to-stop-supporting-foreign-currency-crypto-wallets/, 2026-11-16, search summary). Date: Not found.
- Directive for digital credit service providers and licensing requirements, September 2026 (https://www.bog.gov.gh/notice/publication-of-directive-for-digital-credit-services-providers-and-licensing-requirements/, 2026-11-16). Contents: Not found.
- Remittances: updated inward remittance guidelines and a 24-hour payout rule reported August 2025 (https://www.ghanaweb.com/GhanaHomePage/business/BoG-mandates-24-hour-remittance-payouts-new-FX-rules-1998152, 2026-11-16, search result title only).
- A search summary says non-compliant operators face a deadline after which partnerships with banks and PSPs become "null and void". Source page and date: Not found.

## Tax and fiscal changes affecting merchants
- VAT Act, 2025 (Act 1151) effective 2026-01-01, with phased move from manual receipts to E-VAT and Fiscal Electronic Devices (https://www.vatupdate.com/2026/02/04/ghana-launches-e-vat-system-and-vat-reward-scheme-to-modernize-tax-compliance-in-2026-2, 2026-11-16, search summary).
- Reported, not confirmed: GRA to extend mandatory E-VAT to Tier-2 medium enterprises from 2026-11-01 (https://www.fiscal-requirements.com/news/6142-ghana-clarifies-digital-vat-collection-rules-as-further-e-vat-expansion-is-reported-for-november-2026, 2026-11-16). No GRA notice found.
- Real-time VAT on cross-border digital services: banks, MoMo operators and payment providers withhold VAT at the point of payment when the instrument is Ghana-issued and the merchant is non-resident (https://kpmg.com/us/en/taxnewsflash/news/2026/10/tnf-ghana-real-time-vat-collection-on-cross-border-digital-services.html, 2026-10, checked 2026-11-16). Start date: Not found.

## Market structure
- CEO: Hubtel processes "just about 12% of the volume of transactions" and "This economy has over 800,000 such businesses." (MyJoyOnline 2025-10-04 and Citi Newsroom 2025-10-02, URLs in company.md).
- Mobile money dominated by MTN MoMo (comparison sources, search summary, 2026-11-16).
- Hubtel is a private company licensed by BoG, so Notice 43 applies to Hubtel directly. INFERRED: it is also a trust argument. Hubtel can sell merchant-funds segregation as a feature, or buyers may worry about their PSP.
- INFERRED: Notice 43, the Zeepay revocation and the systemically important designations all push buyers to ask whether their payment provider is licensed and holds funds safely.
