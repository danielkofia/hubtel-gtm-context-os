# Hubtel: competitors (raw research)

Checked: 2026-11-16. Format: fact, then (source URL, date checked). INFERRED marks my own reading. Competitor fee numbers come mostly from comparison blogs and conflict. None were checked against the vendor's own pricing page unless stated.

## Who sells to the same buyers in Ghana
Names appearing across comparison pages: Paystack, Flutterwave, ExpressPay, Slydepay, with iPay, DPO, Theteller and MTN MoMo's own merchant products also listed (https://learnwithhasan.com/payment-gateways/country/ghana/, https://thefinrate.com/top-payment-gateways-in-ghana/, https://paymentproviders.io/country/gh, 2026-11-16, via search results; pages not opened).

## Paystack
- Page title: "Modern online and offline payments for Africa" (https://paystack.com/gh/?localeUpdate=true, 2026-11-16, from search result title). The site itself returned HTTP 403 to the fetch tool, so its own wording: Not found beyond that title.
- Third party: default choice for domestic payments in Ghana; flat 1.95% on local cards and mobile money; T+1 payouts; MTN, Vodafone, AirtelTigo supported; Stripe owned per one guide (https://learnwithhasan.com/payment-gateways/paystack/ and other comparison pages, 2026-11-16, search summary). Founded 2015 by Shola Akinlade and Ezra Olubi (same summary).
- Kolonell (2026-08-24) estimates Paystack at about 3.0% all-in with no fixed fee, T+2 to T+3 settlement, and says "Hubtel for large baskets, Paystack for small recurring ones." (https://kolonell.com/en/blog/hubtel-vs-paystack-aggregator-accra-2026, 2026-11-16). Author's own estimates, no external source.
- Conflict: 1.95% vs about 3.0%. Not reconciled.

## Flutterwave
- Headline: "Endless possibilities for every business" (page title). Main heading: "Endless possibilities for all." Tagline: "Empowering businesses of all sizes with seamless payment solutions tailored for enterprises, startups, and emerging markets." (https://flutterwave.com, 2026-11-16)
- Products listed: Accept online payments, Payout & Transfers, Swap, POS systems, Online checkout, Payment Links, Invoices, Afritickets, Capital, Grow, Card Issuing, FaaS (https://flutterwave.com, 2026-11-16).
- Pricing: none on the page. Third party: 2.6% card and 2% mobile money in Ghana; another table says 2.5% mobile money (search summary, 2026-11-16). Stronger for multi-country African businesses (same summary).

## ExpressPay
- Headline: "The way to go". Tagline: "Send money and make purchases with approved merchants." (https://expresspaygh.com, 2026-11-16)
- Products: "3rd Party API Integration", "Scan to Pay (Quick Response Codes)", "Bill Payments", "Point of Sale Devices", "Bulk Payments", "USSD", "Send Money", "Airtime Top Up".
- Own claims: "API integration with expressPay is the best deal for business owners." "PCI Certified - Nothing is more important to us than protecting your data." "expressPay provides bill pay services to Banks, Utility services providers and more."
- Pricing: none on page. Third party: GHc500 setup and 3% fees in one table (search summary). Founded 2012 by Curtis Vanderpuije (same summary).
- INFERRED: the bill-pay-to-banks-and-utilities pitch overlaps Hubtel's Pay Bills and utility case study.

## Slydepay
- Site did not load (connection refused on http://www.slydepay.com/). Own wording: Not found.
- Third party: founded 2011 by DreamOval, first called iWallet; payment links; 2% mobile money and 3% cards in one fee table (search summary, 2026-11-16).

## Others
- MTN MoMo direct merchant products: Kolonell publishes "MTN MoMo vs Hubtel fees" (https://kolonell.com/en/blog/mtn-momo-vs-hubtel-fees-accra-ghana-2026, 2026-11-16). Body not read.
- Stripe: reached in Ghana via Paystack, card-first, no native MoMo channel per one comparison (search summary, 2026-11-16).
- Zeepay: BoG revoked its licence July 2026 over e-money violations (https://www.myjoyonline.com/bank-of-ghana-revokes-zeepays-licence-over-e-money-violations/, 2026-11-16). Relevant to buyer risk, see market.md.

## How Hubtel positions against them
- Own words: "FOR SCALE-READY BUSINESSES IN GHANA" and not for those who want "the cheapest fee" (https://hubtel.com, 2026-11-16).
- Third party view of Hubtel: all-in-one platform for SMEs, strong in Ghana, weak cross-border (one source, unverified).
- CEO says Hubtel handles "just about 12%" of transaction volume (see company.md).
- No head-to-head comparison page published by Hubtel found. Not found.
- Comparison sites with commercial interest (Kolonell sells payment integration) may bias numbers.
