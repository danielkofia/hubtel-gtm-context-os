# Hubtel: company (raw research)

Checked: 2026-11-16. Format: fact, then (source URL, date checked). INFERRED marks my own reading. Quotes are as returned by the fetch tool and may be trimmed.

## What they sell
- Headline on the homepage: "When nothing blocks payments, growth happens." (https://hubtel.com, 2026-11-16)
- Audience line: "FOR SCALE-READY BUSINESSES IN GHANA" (https://hubtel.com, 2026-11-16)
- Homepage nav groups: Everyday Business (Clarity, Control, Growth, Switch to Hubtel, Developer APIs); Bills, Food & Stores (Food, Stores, Pay Bills, Send SMS, Pay Small Small); Serve with Us (Sell on Hubtel, Ride & Earn on Hubtel); Apps (BackOffice App) (https://hubtel.com, 2026-11-16)
- Payment channels shown: MTN MoMo, Telecel Cash, AT Money, bank, cash, POS, card, invoice link, split bill. Logos shown for GhQR, Mastercard, Visa, GhIPSS, Affinity (https://hubtel.com, 2026-11-16)
- Developer APIs named in case studies: Direct Debit API, Receive & Send Money API, Send Money API, SMS and Notification API, USSD deposits (https://news.hubtel.com/2025/09/ and search summaries, 2026-11-16)
- Stanford GSB profile: "We're digitizing storefronts across Africa by providing a single service" to "help businesses sell in-store, online, and on mobile." Mentions tools for banks, telecoms, utilities and retailers (https://www.gsb.stanford.edu/seed/companies-leaders/meet-entrepreneurs/company-spotlights/hubtel, 2026-11-16). Date of profile: Not found, likely older.
- LinkedIn description: "Find and pay for everyday essentials on Ghana's most useful app. Fast same-day deliveries. 30 minutes for most orders." LinkedIn tagline: "Building a platform that everyone can find, pay and easily partake in the digital economy." Specialities listed: sms, mobile VAS, Voice, USSD, Mobile communication, Technology, mobile payments, ecommerce (https://www.linkedin.com/company/hubtel, via treg fetchinio.linkedin.company.profile, 2026-11-16)
- INFERRED: Hubtel is two businesses in one brand: a B2B payments and messaging platform, and a consumer super-app (food, stores, bills, rides). The GTM question is which one to sell.

## Pricing model
- Homepage: no prices listed. The fetch tool reported the page says Hubtel is not for businesses that want "the cheapest fee" (https://hubtel.com, 2026-11-16). Exact wording needs a manual check.
- Hubtel's own page text, as returned by search: "Simple transparent transaction fee of 1.95% per transaction value. This could be absorbed by your business or passed onto the beneficiary wallet." (Hubtel page surfaced in search, exact URL not opened; see https://explore.hubtel.com/legal/service-fees/, 2026-11-16)
- Service fees page (https://explore.hubtel.com/legal/service-fees/): fee table did not load for the fetch tool ("Loading legal documents..."). Fee table: Not found.
- CEO: "When we serve small businesses, we charge upfront." (https://www.myjoyonline.com/hubtel-ceo-reveals-110m-annual-revenue-sees-huge-growth-potential-in-ghanas-fintech-sector/, 2026-11-16)
- Third party figures conflict: 2.5% + about 100 FCFA fixed (Kolonell, 2026-08-24, https://kolonell.com/en/blog/hubtel-vs-paystack-aggregator-accra-2026); 1.5 to 2% (other Kolonell post); 1% (one POS case study per search summary). Treat all as unverified.
- Terms update announced 2025-12-22 adds an "Actions by Hubtel" clause: may block settlement, suspend or terminate, charge fees or penalties "with or without prior notice" (https://news.hubtel.com/2025/12/, 2026-11-16)

## Markets
- Ghana only on the homepage: "8+ Cities in Ghana" (https://hubtel.com, 2026-11-16). HQ: 302 Olympics Road, Kokomlemle, Accra (LinkedIn profile, 2026-11-16).
- Earlier regional ambition: Stanford profile mentions new offices in Nairobi for Kenya. A web search summary said Hubtel closed offices in Kenya, Nigeria and Cameroon to focus on Ghana. I did not open the source for that. Treat as unverified.
- Stated direction: "Pursuing cross-border expansion and pan-African growth" and "Africa's most useful company" (https://www.myjoyonline.com/hubtel-strengthens-africa-expansion-drive-with-new-advisory-council-appointments/, 2025-09-11, checked 2026-11-16). LinkedIn 2026-10-29: "our ambitions beyond Ghana" (https://www.linkedin.com/posts/hubtel_we-were-delighted-to-host-a-delegation-from-activity-7521530741822742529-7nwe, 2026-11-16).

## Scale claims (self-reported unless noted)
- "7,979+" Businesses, "4M+" Annual Users, "8+" Cities (https://hubtel.com, 2026-11-16)
- 20th anniversary release (April 2025): 722 staff, over 8,000 businesses, 12 million customers, 932 delivery riders, eight cities, nearly GHS 160 billion processed as of 2023 (https://news.hubtel.com/2025/04, 2026-11-16)
- CEO to CNN, reported 2025-10-04: "over $110 million in annual revenue"; "Even with the number of fintechs in the country, we still process just about 12% of the volume of transactions" (https://www.myjoyonline.com/hubtel-ceo-reveals-110m-annual-revenue-sees-huge-growth-potential-in-ghanas-fintech-sector/, 2026-11-16)
- CEO: "This economy has over 800,000 such businesses." (https://www.citinewsroom.com/2025/10/cnn-spotlights-hubtels-rise-as-ghanas-fintech-leader/, 2025-10-02, checked 2026-11-16)
- A less established source claims 15,000 merchants and 1M+ daily transactions (https://villpress.com/?p=6473, 2026-11-16). Single source, unverified.
- Conflict: 7,979+ vs 8,000+ vs 15,000 businesses. Not reconciled.
