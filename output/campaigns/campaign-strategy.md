## Read this first

1. **In Ghana, outbound is account-based, not signal-led.** Both signal tests came back thin: hiring gave 13 postings, 5 in Ghana, 0 in an ICP segment; tenders gave 0 of 119 about payments. The register gave 47 named online brands. The real universe is dozens of accounts per segment, not thousands. Most ideas below are lists of 5 to 50 accounts, sent by hand-checked, high-research messages.
2. **Copy is blocked on facts, not on data.** `facts.md` has no approved claim and no authorised customer name. Ideas that need a claim (proof, numbers, Notice 43) cannot ship until Hubtel approves one. Ideas marked "no claim needed" can.
3. **Persona titles are open.** The skill's New Hire idea depends on knowing which title to watch. Ask Hubtel first.
4. **A date to plan around.** Every licence on the Gaming Commission register runs 1 Jan to 31 Dec 2026 (one row ends 31 Jan 2026). Expect one renewal wave in January 2027, about six weeks away. A monthly diff of the list will show new and dropped brands then. Whether the register publishes quickly is Not found.

## Ranking method

Score S (signal strength 0 to 3: how strongly the filter says "needs this now") plus D (data ease 0 to 3: can we detect it reliably and cheaply today). Rank = S + D, ties broken by volume. Tier is build order and also weighs volume, build time and differentiation. A high score with a blocker is still Tier 2 until the blocker clears.

## Idea cards

### 1. New gaming licence wave (Tier 1: tested source, small list, high value per win)
- **Mother intent:** Niche-specific play (licensing events). Not in the catalog, so flagged as a new intent.
- **Segment and persona:** Segment 3, betting and gaming. Economic buyer INFERRED: country head or finance head. Champion INFERRED: head of payments or product. Titles Not found.
- **Signal and source:** A brand appears on, or returns to, the Gaming Commission licensed-operators page. Source: the register, read with `treg.web.extract` ($0.00025 per read, the site certificate is expired so the normal fetch tool refuses it). We hold the baseline: 88 licence rows, 47 named online or remote brands, all running 1 Jan to 31 Dec 2026. Diff monthly with n8n.
- **Angle:** INFERRED: a newly licensed operator needs working Ghana deposits and payouts fast. Lead with channels and USSD for users without smartphones (pillar 4). No claim until approved. Hedge: "my guess is".
- **Volume:** 0 to 3 new brands a month, plus a January 2027 batch. Not found: how fast the register updates.
- **Difficulty:** Medium. The register has no domains or company details, so name to company resolution is the main risk. Untested. The proposed 10-brand test answers it.
- **Why it is different:** No other PSP in the research reads this register. Tested, cheap, bounded.

### 2. Utility revenue-pressure ABM (Tier 1: strongest public pain, Golden ICP)
- **Mother intent:** Regulatory changes (revenue under public scrutiny).
- **Segment and persona:** Segment 1, utilities. Candidate accounts INFERRED and WEB, to verify: ECG, NEDCo, Ghana Water Limited, and similar. Economic buyer INFERRED: finance or commercial director. Champion INFERRED: head of revenue assurance or IT.
- **Signal and source:** Public pressure on collections. WEB: an IMF 2026 paper reports ECG declared GH¢10.4B against validated collections of GH¢15.5B, and PURC probed prepaid credit depletion in Feb 2026 (both from signal-matrix N3, not in research). Source: news, PURC, the IMF paper. Tool: treg web search.
- **Angle:** One platform for every pay-in channel (pillar 1). Use the manual research a rep would do in 10 minutes: one fact about the account's own public collections story, then a question. Never diagnose their problem as failing (copy ban).
- **Volume:** 3 to 6 accounts, once. Each is a large deal.
- **Difficulty:** Medium. Danger: the power distributor in Hubtel's own case study may be ECG (INFERRED, unconfirmed). Ask Hubtel before touching any utility so we do not cold-email an existing customer.
- **Why it is different:** The only list where the prospect's pain is already public.

## No-AI campaign

**Licensed operator, one question** (idea 4, gaming).
- Why it works without AI: the register is the personalization. Every brand is a verified licensee, so the line is true by construction.
- Core message structure: three to four short lines. Line 1: "{{brand}} is on the 2026 Gaming Commission list." Line 2: one sentence on deposits and payouts through USSD and APIs. Line 3: one question, "who looks after payments for {{brand}} in Ghana?" No claim until `facts.md` is approved.

## Front-end offers

1. **Collection channel check.** A one-page note of which pay-in channels an organisation's public pages accept (MTN MoMo, Telecel Cash, AT Money, card, USSD, invoice link) and one gap. Built from public data, no Hubtel claim needed. Fits ideas 2, 5 and 9. The prospect sees value first.
2. **Pilot on one channel.** Try one pay-in channel on one product line before a full switch. Hubtel pilot terms are Not found, so ask.
3. **Notice 43 funds-handling note.** Where merchant funds sit and what the notice asks. On hold until Hubtel confirms its position.

## Hand-off

Recommended first workflow: ideas 1 and 4 together (gaming), as already decided. Run `/build-workflow-spec` on it after the 10-brand name-resolution test. Idea 2 (utilities) is the second workflow. Idea 5 can run beside either, because it needs no claim.
